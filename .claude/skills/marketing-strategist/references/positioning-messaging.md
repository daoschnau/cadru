# Phase 1 — Positioning & messaging

Positioning is the spine; every page, email, and post inherits from it. Lock this before touching copy. Ground everything in `cadru-context.md`.

## 1. Positioning statement

Fill this in explicitly (April Dunford style):

- **For** [primary ICP] — e.g. the lone in-house jurist / owner-operator of a small MD company
- **who** [situation/struggle] — contracts scattered across folders and inboxes, copy-pasted from old files, deadlines slipping
- **Cadru is a** [category] — practical contract-management system for small teams
- **that** [key benefit] — brings order and discipline to contracts without a legal department or heavy software
- **unlike** [alternative] — Excel-plus-folders, a foreign enterprise CLM, or hiring a lawyer
- **because** [differentiators] — built by a practicing jurisconsult, deterministic (not AI-hype), local QES signing, sized for small teams.

**Category note:** "CLM" is jargon here. Use the outcome in the headline ("Contracte sub control"), introduce the category only as clarification. Don't make buyers learn a new acronym before they feel the pain.

## 2. ICP — go narrow

Define **one primary** ICP and at most one secondary. In a small market, a sharp "who it's for" outperforms a broad one. For each:
- Role & context, the trigger event that makes them look, where they already hang out, who else is in the buying decision.
- **Disqualifiers** — who looks like a fit but isn't. Naming these saves the founder's scarcest resource (time).

The founder embodies the primary ICP (jurisconsult). Use that: "built by one of you."

## 3. Job-to-be-done

Write the JTBD as the buyer would feel it, not as a feature list:

> "When my contracts are scattered and I'm copy-pasting from last year's file and I've already missed one renewal, I want to get everything under control **without** hiring a legal team or learning complex software — so I stop losing time, money, and sleep over avoidable mistakes."

Everything on the site should ladder back to relieving this.

## 4. Differentiators & anti-positioning

**Differentiators (defensible, specific):**
- Built by a practicing jurisconsult *for* practitioners — credibility in a trust category.
- **Deterministic, not generative** — "AI ≠ automation; the 50-cent script." Anti-hype stance is itself positioning in 2026.
- Local **QES** signing — works with MD reality, not a foreign afterthought.
- Right-sized for small teams — not an enterprise CLM you'll never fully use.
- **Built in public** — you watch it being made; you can shape it.

**Anti-positioning (what we are *not*):**
- Not another bloated enterprise CLM.
- Not "AI will magically handle your contracts" — deterministic, human-in-control.
- Not a law firm / not legal advice — a tool for discipline.

Anti-positioning is a feature: it repels the wrong buyers and bonds the right ones.

## 5. Message hierarchy

Every surface follows this ladder, in order:

1. **Promise (one line):** the outcome — "Contracts under control, without a whole legal department."
2. **Mechanism:** *how* — the 4 modules (database → forms → templates → automations) and the Crawl·Walk·Run path. Make the abstract concrete.
3. **Proof:** *why believe you* — the founder's authority, the workshop, design-partner results (as they exist), the deterministic philosophy.
4. **Offer:** *what to do now* — the single next action (join early access / get the lead magnet / become a design partner), matched to funnel stage.

If a piece of copy doesn't map to one of these four, cut it.

## 6. Messaging by maturity stage (reuse Crawl·Walk·Run)

The product's own maturity model doubles as audience segmentation — meet buyers where they are:
- **Crawl** (contracts are chaos): message = "just get your contracts in one place, low commitment." Lead magnet territory.
- **Walk** (some order, want standards): message = "standardize with templates + special conditions (DRY)."
- **Run** (ready to automate): message = "real automation, data → document, QES signing."

This lets the same product speak to a cold owner and a sophisticated jurist without diluting either.

## Output

A **GTM Strategy → Positioning** section in `marketing/strategy.md` containing: the filled positioning statement, primary/secondary ICP with disqualifiers, the JTBD, the differentiator + anti-positioning list, and the message hierarchy. This becomes the copy brief for every downstream deliverable.
