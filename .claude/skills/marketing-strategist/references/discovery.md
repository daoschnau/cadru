# Phase 0 — Discovery & diagnosis

A real strategist earns the right to prescribe by diagnosing first. The goal of this phase is a **Situation Assessment**, not a plan. Ask for what you don't already know from `cadru-context.md`. Batch questions; don't interrogate one at a time. Where a number doesn't exist yet, mark it "to instrument" rather than guessing.

## What you must find out

Group the questions; ask the whole group at once.

### 1. Goals & timeline
- What does "success" look like in 3 / 6 / 12 months? (e.g. "10 design partners", "first 20 paying customers", "1,000 qualified emails")
- Is there a target launch date, or is it "when it's ready"?
- Is the near-term goal **revenue**, **learning/validation**, or **audience**? (These imply different motions.)

### 2. Current traction (get real numbers)
- Current email/waitlist size? Growth rate?
- Site traffic (monthly visitors, sources)? Any analytics installed?
- Workshop attendance — how many, how often, what's the follow-up today?
- Any existing customers, pilots, or letters of intent?
- Founder's LinkedIn / social following and posting cadence?

### 3. Assets & capacity
- How many hours/week can the founder realistically give to marketing?
- Anyone else on the team (design, dev, content, sales)?
- Budget for tools/ads (even rough)?
- Existing content that can be repurposed (workshop decks, recordings, articles, templates)?

### 4. Offer & economics
- Intended pricing / model (subscription? per-seat? one-time?) — even a guess.
- Who signs the check vs. who uses it?
- What would a "design partner" get, and what do you want from them?

### 5. Positioning inputs
- Who do buyers compare Cadru to today? (Excel + folders? A foreign CLM? A lawyer? Nothing?)
- Top 1–2 objections you already hear.
- What makes a prospect a *great* fit vs. a time-sink?

### 6. Constraints & risks
- Anything off-limits (channels, claims, competitors, naming — remember "Cadru" is provisional)?
- Regulatory/compliance sensitivities in how you can market legal software in MD?
- Biggest fear about the launch?

## The Situation Assessment (Phase 0 output)

Produce a **one-page** doc with exactly these sections:

1. **Where we are** — traction, assets, list size, authority position (2–4 bullets).
2. **The funnel today** — trace a real visitor's path and name where it leaks (e.g. "lands → reads → maybe joins list → then *nothing* happens"). Be specific about the biggest leak.
3. **The single biggest constraint** — the one thing that, if unblocked, unlocks the most (usually: founder time, no nurture after opt-in, or no proof).
4. **Recommended motion** — one paragraph: the overall GTM shape you're proposing and *why it fits this market* (expect: authority-led + build-in-public + design-partner-led sales).
5. **What we'll instrument** — the numbers that don't exist yet but must, before we can steer.

Keep it to one page. Confirm it with the founder before moving to Phase 1 — if the diagnosis is wrong, everything downstream is wrong.
